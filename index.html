Sustainable Cafe System

Rental Cups + Online Ordering

The purpose is simple: reduce single-use cups and daily cafe waste by making reusable cups easier to use.

Problem

Too many disposable coffee cups end up as street rubbish. The system focuses on practical sustainability, not just branding.

Core Idea

Combine the things customers actually need: water, coffee, convenience, and a reusable cup system that is easy to repeat.

Goal

Make reusable cup use simple enough to become part of normal cafe service and reduce single-use packaging over time.

How the Rental Cup System Works

01Scan NFCCustomer scans the rental cup with a phone.
02Order OnlineThe scan connects directly to the online ordering platform.
03Return CupThe customer returns the rental cup when ordering.
04Save 30¢Rental cup return + order gives a 30¢ discount.

Steaming Basics

Open this only when you need the steaming steps.

Steaming training video

Detailed flow: purge, stretch, texture, finish.

1. Purge + Position

Purge first. Place the tip just under the surface and slightly off-centre.

2. Stretch

Add air for 2–3 seconds. Listen for a soft “tss tss”, not loud bubbling.

3. Texture

Lower the tip slightly. Create a strong whirlpool until glossy.

1Purge the steam wand before and after steaming.
2Use your full hand on the jug to feel the temperature.
3Stop around 55–60°C, when the jug is too hot to comfortably hold.
4Tap and swirl the jug before pouring. Milk should look like wet paint, not bubbly.

Service Flow Tips

Compact visual guide for busy service.

1. Milk-based → Water-based

Milk-basedlatte / flat white / cappuccino
→
Water-basedlong black
Reason: long black does not need steaming, so the person pulling shots can finish it quickly. Milk drinks need steaming time, so start them first.

2. Small latte → Large latte

S
Small latteless milk, faster steaming
→
L
Large latteuse remaining milk
Reason: small drinks finish faster, and the remaining milk can be reused or adjusted more easily for a large drink.

3. 3-shot drinks

1
1 shot firstsingle shot first
→
2
Add 2 shotsdouble shot next
Reason: if the cup has only 1 shot, it is visually clear that 2 more shots are still needed. If 2 shots go in first, it is harder to tell whether the drink already has 2 or 3 shots.
